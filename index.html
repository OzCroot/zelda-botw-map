Breath of the Wild Interactive Map

A free interactive map for The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild. Browse and filter Korok seeds, shrines, towers, stables, Ancient Tech Labs, quests, locations and more, and track your 100% completion progress in your browser. No account needed; your progress stays on your device.

Features

  • Search every marker and filter by category
  • Mark locations complete and undo with one tap
  • Export, import and share your progress with a share code
